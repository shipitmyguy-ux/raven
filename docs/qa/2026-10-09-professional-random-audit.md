## Prioritized action plan

1. **P1 — Establish a role-evidence plan before writing.** Extract actual duties/requirements separately from company marketing. Map them to employer-specific facts, general transferable facts and explicit gaps. Select the strongest supported examples; put general project delivery and internal meetings in separate highlights rather than assigning them to a past employer. Retain original titles/dates and concise honest industry context. Test low-fit roles without manufacturing domain experience.
2. **P1 — Separate factuality, tailoring and fit in review.** Check actual quoted claims against evidence; reject art-dominated/generic narrative when better relevant evidence exists. Missing desired SaaS, finance or algorithm credentials are fit gaps, not by themselves fabricated claims. Do not fix relevance through invented titles, years, tools or duties. Use full-output comparisons and guard against both false passes and false rejection.
3. **P1 — Make failures diagnosable and durable.** Preserve private rejected candidate/quoted-review evidence for authorized QA, without marking it approved or replacing saved documents. Distinguish provider timeout/schema/reviewer failure from unsupported claims and role-fit gaps. Persist per-document outcomes through existing activity/state so refresh retains the reason. Saved job must not imply saved documents.
4. **P2 — Verify the complete one-button path with agreed acceptance.** Both documents must be role-specific, grounded, reviewable, correctly linked, visually clean and unchanged after reload. Preserve a successful document if the other fails; retry only the failure after its cause is fixed. Conventional cover closing, no generic filler or repeated prose. Word/PDF checks are separate from HTML persistence.
5. **P2 — Clarify actions and location scope.** Make built-in creation versus external ChatGPT handoff clear. Keep DataHouse hidden; tighten hybrid eligibility to Fort Collins only rather than introducing saved-job exceptions.

No further changes from this plan have been implemented. Existing writer v5 safeguards are deployed, but output-quality acceptance remains open. Personal evidence stays ignored under private/; committed report contains no complete resume or credentials.
